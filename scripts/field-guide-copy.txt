Title：What Is Azeroth Survivors? Free Browser Roguelike Explained

Meta Description：What Azeroth Survivors is: a free, fan-made WoW-style browser roguelike by Aethus. WebGPU requirement, 9 classes, 16-minute runs, no account needed.

H1：What is Azeroth Survivors?

正文
第一段（直接回答）：

Azeroth Survivors is a free, fan-made browser roguelike created by Aethus. It uses World of Warcraft's classes, zones and spell names, but it is not a Blizzard product and not connected to the WoW client. You open it in a WebGPU-capable browser, pick a race and a class, and survive a roughly 16-minute zone run where your abilities cast themselves and your only inputs are movement and level-up card choices.[reference:1]

第二段（它不是什么）：

It is not a private server, not a WoW addon, and not a download-and-install MMO. The whole run happens locally in your browser; there is no account system and no persistent online character. Two CurseForge addons with similar names run inside WoW Forever and are different games — do not confuse them with this browser roguelike.[reference:2]

第三段（核心循环）：

The loop is survivors-like: enemies spawn in waves, your spells fire automatically, and every level-up deals three cards — a new spell, a rank-up, or a passive. Cards are color-coded by estimated damage, but color is a hint, not a guarantee. You can reroll the hand with R. Between runs, you keep boss gear, collection progress and a 51-point talent tree that can be reset.[reference:3]

第四段（门槛）：

The real requirement is WebGPU. Chrome and Edge are the safe starting point; Safari works on 26 and above. It is a keyboard-and-mouse game — mobile is not a supported target. First load pulls the whole world into the browser, so a slow first load is expected; repeated reloading makes it worse.[reference:4][reference:5]

Title：How to Play Azeroth Survivors — First Run Walkthrough

Meta Description：Step-by-step first run for Azeroth Survivors: browser setup, class choice, the 0–3 / 3–8 / 8–15 minute rule, reroll timing and the first boss.

H1：How to play Azeroth Survivors

正文（分步骤，文案直接可用）
Step 1 — Open the maintained entry point.

Go to Aethus's own site. Use a current Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. If the loader says your browser has no WebGPU, update the browser first, then enable hardware/GPU acceleration. Click inside the game canvas before you try any key; without focus, keyboard input will not register.[reference:6]

Step 2 — Pick a class, not a "best" class.

For a first clear, Warrior or Paladin. Warrior has the largest health pool and a Whirlwind capstone that turns into blender mode; Paladin brings Consecration, shields, self-heal and a once-per-90-second death save. Both forgive positioning mistakes while your talent rows are still locked. Hunter, Warlock and Mage have higher power ceilings, but they ask you to know enemy movement before they pay off.[reference:7]

Step 3 — Minutes 0–3: take wave-clear only.

Your first six levels are about turning the spawn curve into experience. Take area spells — Blizzard, Volley, Consecration, Rain of Fire, Chain Lightning. If you started a single-target class, your first area tool has to be online before the first boss at around 2:15.[reference:8]

Step 4 — Minutes 3–8: commit to one carry.

Push your best area spell toward rank 8 and add exactly one survival tool — a slow, a stun, or a movement passive. The mobility spell lands around this window. Spend your three rerolls here, in the level 6–12 range, because a bad hand at this point costs the most tempo.[reference:9]

Step 5 — Minutes 8–15: rank-ups only.

Your slots are full. Every card is now a stat line: damage % on the carry first, cooldown % second, survival last. Bosses resist control at this stage — burst windows beat CC chains.[reference:10]

Step 6 — Between runs.

Check Collection, Armory and Talents before you queue again. Boss gear is permanent for that class, and the armory is where you see what your class can actually find.[reference:11]

Title：Azeroth Survivors Download — Browser, Windows & Fixes

Meta Description：How to get Azeroth Survivors: browser play via WebGPU, Windows build, and fixes for slow first load, black screen and no-WebGPU warnings. No mirrors hosted here.

H1：Azeroth Survivors download and first-run fixes

正文
浏览器版本：

The maintained way to play is the browser build on Aethus's own site. You need Chrome or Edge on desktop, WebGPU enabled, and patience on first load. The game caches the world locally, so the first pull is the slow one; after that, repeat opens are faster. If the loader reports no WebGPU, update the browser and check that hardware acceleration is on — do not download a "fix" from a third-party site for this.[reference:12]

Windows 版本：

A Windows build exists for players who would rather not keep a browser tab pinned. It is distributed through the developer's own channels. Treat any.rarnamed after the game on a third-party domain as unverified: the filename pattern has been circulated by mirror and aggregator sites, not by the developer. We do not link to or host it.

黑屏 / 卡加载：

Black screen at launch is almost always a WebGPU or focus problem, not a corrupted install. Close other GPU-heavy tabs, confirm hardware acceleration, reload once, click inside the canvas after the loader finishes. If the screen stays black after the loader clears, switch from Chrome to Edge or vice versa before you try anything more drastic.

慢下载：

The first resource pull is the largest. A slow first load is not a symptom of a bad download; repeated reloading restarts the cache and makes it slower. Leave the tab open until the in-game prompt appears.

H1：Is Azeroth Survivors safe to download?

正文
直接回答：

The official entry point — Aethus's own domain — has passed Cloudflare Radar scanning with no malicious-content finding. That is a point-in-time technical result, not a lifetime guarantee. The honest answer is: safe from the official source is the supported path; safe from a random mirror is not something anyone can promise you.

为什么第三方站会被标记：

Independent fan sites around this game have been reviewed as "low risk but not safe" for two structural reasons, not for malware: the domain is very new, and the page carries third-party advertising and sponsored outbound links. The scanner found no phishing, no payment fraud and no malicious payload — but a 13-day-old domain with ad slots cannot be rated the same way as a long-standing official site.[reference:14]

假站识别清单（直接列表）：

The page offers a.rardownload instead of sending you to the developer's site or an official build.

The domain is a near-miss of the game name — extra hyphen, plural,.orgor.shopinstead of the real one.

The site embeds a different browser game on the same page to keep you there.

It asks for payment, account credentials or a "verification" download before play.

It redirects you away from the developer's domain when you click Play.

下载前检查清单（直接列表）：

Confirm the domain matches the entry point you already know.

Do not enter personal or payment details on a fan page.

Verify any sponsored outbound link before downloading from it.

If you already have progress, note that switching domains can wipe local save data — this is a known consequence of moving between mirrors and the official site.

H1：Azeroth Survivors builds: the rule that actually decides runs

正文
核心规则（第一段直接放）：

Every level-up deals three cards. What you take in which order decides the run, not which class you picked. The working rule is simple: minutes 0–3 take wave-clear, minutes 3–8 commit to one carry spell plus one survival tool, after minute 8 take rank-ups only.[reference:15]

被动优先级（做成有序列表，直接可用）：

1. Blessing of Might — +10% all damage per rank, +50% at rank 5. It helps every build; take it first.[reference:16]
2. Arcane Intellect — 8% faster recharge per rank, 40% at rank 5, total capped at 65%.[reference:17]
3. Amplify Magic — +10% area size per rank, +50% at rank 5. Huge for Flamestrike, Consecration, Volley.[reference:18]
4. Blessing of Wisdom — +8% experience per rank, +40% at rank 5. Take it early or not at all.[reference:19]
5. Power Word: Fortitude — +20% max health per rank, +100% at rank 5. Buffer on squishier classes.[reference:20]

属性上限（三个短段，直接可用）：

Armor caps at 60%. Once the panel shows 60% reduction, flat Armor stops helping; switch to max health, dodge, shield or regen.[reference:21]

Crit past 100% is not wasted — overflow converts into bonus critical damage.[reference:22]

Echo past 100% rolls a third strike: the first 100% guarantees a second cast on every spell, and the overflow rolls for the third.[reference:23]

Title：Azeroth Survivors Hunter Build — Explosive Trap & Beast Mastery

Meta Description：Hunter build for Azeroth Survivors: Explosive Trap / Beast Mastery shell, early area coverage, pet value and when to switch to single-target.

H1：Azeroth Survivors Hunter build

正文首段：

Hunter is the safest S-tier pick in the current alpha because it does not need to stand in the wave to clear it. The community shell is Explosive Trap for area denial plus Beast Mastery for free pet damage while you keep moving. You are not the fastest screen-clear; you are the class that keeps its damage uptime while repositioning, which is why it holds up across zones and bosses alike.[reference:31][reference:32]

Build phases（直接写，不泛泛）：

0–3 min：Take Volley if it appears; otherwise take any area card to turn the spawn curve into experience. Do not commit to single-target yet.

3–8 min：Commit to Explosive Trap as the carry. Add exactly one survival tool. Beast Mastery is the preferred second commitment because the pet keeps working while you dodge.

8–15 min：Rank-ups only on the carry, then cooldown reduction. Bosses resist control; burst windows matter more than extra slows.

Boss notes：Hunter's advantage is that the pet maintains pressure during your repositioning. Keep the boss between you and the pet, and do not burn the mobility spell to chase damage.

Common mistakes：Taking single-target crit cards before Volley or Explosive Trap is online. Standing still to "let the pet tank". Spending rerolls in the first three levels instead of the 6–12 window.

其余职业页的首段文案（直接复制到对应页面）
Paladin：

Paladin is the easiest first clear in the game. Consecration gives you a persistent ground burn, shields and self-heal cover early mistakes, and the once-per-90-second death save means one bad wave does not end the run. It is A-tier rather than S because the power ceiling is lower than Hunter or Warlock, but for a player still learning enemy timing, the floor matters more than the ceiling.[reference:33]

Warlock：

Warlock is an S-tier snowball. DoTs that keep working after the target dies, demon pets, and self-healing mean the class gets stronger the longer a wave lasts. The community shell is Soul Link plus Drain Soul. It is not as immediately safe as Hunter, but once talents come online it scales harder than any other ranged class.[reference:34]

Mage：

Mage has the fastest screen-clear in the alpha and three strong capstones, but no pet to hide behind. Frostbite plus Shatter is the community S-tier shell. Play it if you already know enemy movement; the class punishes a missed reposition more than any other ranged pick.[reference:35][reference:36]

Warrior：

Warrior is the most forgiving melee: the largest health pool, built-in cleave, and a Whirlwind capstone that turns dense waves into free experience. The Arms / Execute / Mortal Strike direction produces very high DPS, but you have to keep moving — standing in caster packs is the one thing Warrior cannot heal through.[reference:37]

Rogue：

Rogue melts bosses and punishes surround mistakes. The burst is real, but the class demands deliberate circling: if you let the wave close from two sides, the health pool does not buy you enough time. It is the pick for players who already read enemy movement, not for a first clear.[reference:38]

Title：What Does Luck Do in Azeroth Survivors?

Meta Description：Luck in Azeroth Survivors shifts the rarity of level-up drafts. It is a draft-quality stat, not a damage stat — here is where it sits in priority and when to take it.

H1：What does Luck do in Azeroth Survivors?

正文（直接可用）：

Luck changes the rarity distribution of the cards you are offered on level-up. It does not add flat damage, and it does not change drop rates from bosses in a way you can measure from a single run. The practical effect is that a higher Luck stat gives you a better chance of seeing higher-quality drafts during the 3–8 minute commitment window, where a bad hand costs the most tempo.[reference:40]

Treat Luck as a support stat, not a carry stat. If your draft already contains the area spell and the single survival tool you need, Luck is a reasonable third priority. If you are still missing your carry by minute 6, Luck is not the card that fixes the run.

Title：Azeroth Survivors Save File — Location, Backup & Risks

Meta Description：Where Azeroth Survivors stores progress, how to back up a save, and why save editors and third-party "full save" files are not worth the risk.

H1：Azeroth Survivors save file

正文：

The browser build stores progress locally. On Windows, character data has been reported under the user profile in a characters.txt file; the exact path depends on how the browser build is packaged.[reference:41] Because the save is local, switching domains or clearing browser storage can wipe progress — this is the same reason players moving between mirrors and the official site have lost runs.[reference:42]

Back up by copying the save file before you change domains, clear cache or try a different build. Do not use a third-party "full save" or save editor unless you are willing to lose the character: the community versions circulate without source and are not distributed by the developer. We do not link to them.

Title：Azeroth Survivors Talent Tree — 51-Point Meta Progression

Meta Description：How the 51-point talent tree works between runs, what resets, what persists, and how to spend early points without wasting a run.

H1：Azeroth Survivors talent tree

正文：

The talent tree is the between-run progression layer: 51 points, resettable, and separate from the in-run level-up draft. Boss gear, collection and talent progress carry across runs; a single run's card choices do not.[reference:44][reference:45]

Spend early points on the stat that fixes your class's weakest phase, not on the stat that doubles down on its strongest phase. A Paladin with low movement benefits more from a defensive or mobility point than from another damage node; a Mage with no survival tool benefits more from a buffer than from a third damage multiplier.

Our job is to explain the game clearly and point you to the verified entry point, not to replace it.

/editorial-policy/ 首段：

Every guide on this site is written from play notes, verifiable community sources and the developer's own published information. We mark alpha-state mechanics as alpha, we date every page, and we correct pages when a balance patch or a confirmed source contradicts what we wrote. If a claim cannot be verified, it does not go in the main body — it goes in a note or it is left out.
